Golf Trips to Turkey

The best-value week of golf in the wider European game, if you go in the right months.

GolfTrip › Destinations › Turkey Golf Trips

Belek is a purpose-built golf resort strip half an hour east of Antalya airport, with a dozen championship courses and a set of five-star all-inclusive hotels sitting between them. The model is different from Spain or Portugal: instead of booking a hotel and buying green fees, you buy a package — seven nights all-inclusive plus three or four rounds — and the golf comes bundled at a price no pay-as-you-play itinerary can match.

The catch is timing. High summer in Antalya reaches the mid-thirties and afternoon golf stops being enjoyable. Belek's real season is March to May and October to November, with a cheap and perfectly playable winter either side. Get the month right and a week here, all food and drink included, costs less than four nights and three rounds in the Algarve.

Where to Play

The Courses Worth Building a Trip Around

Belek

Montgomerie Maxx Royal

The best-regarded course on the strip and priced accordingly. What you pay depends on which hotel you're staying in, plus a per-player administration fee.

Green fee: €110–€260 by season and hotel

Belek

Regnum Carya

Turkish Airlines Open host, floodlit for night golf, and the most immaculate conditioning in Belek. Staying at the Regnum knocks a chunk off the rate.

Green fee: €85–€185 (hotel guests €75–€145)

Belek

PGA Sultan, Antalya Golf Club

The long-standing tournament course — big, strategic and the sternest test on the strip after the Montgomerie.

Green fee: €89–€184

Belek

The Pasha, Antalya Golf Club

The Sultan's shorter, tighter neighbour. The most society-friendly course in Belek and often the cheapest round of the week.

Green fee: €69–€149

Belek

Cornelia (Faldo)

Twenty-seven holes of Nick Faldo design through the pines, with three nines that can be combined in different ways.

Green fee: €107–€149

Belek

Gloria Old & New

Two established courses in one resort, water in play on both, and one of the most reliable value packages in Belek.

Green fee: €102–€154 each

Belek

Kaya Palazzo

A tough, well-conditioned layout attached to one of the better-value all-inclusive resorts.

Green fee: €99–€145

Belek

National Golf Club

The original Belek course, David Feherty and Donald Steel, and still the members' favourite. Tree-lined and fair.

Green fee: €89–€129

Antalya

Lykia Links

A genuine links-style layout an hour west of Belek. Different golf from everything else in the region and cheap with it.

Green fee: €69–€139

Green fees in Belek move a lot with the season, and most courses charge a different rate depending on whether you're staying in an affiliated hotel. Rack rates like these are the wrong way to price a Turkey trip — price the package instead.

Timing

When to Go

MonthsTypical daytimeWhat to expect
March–May19–26°CPeak golf season. Courses at their best, prices at their highest, tee sheets busy. Book two to four weeks ahead minimum.
June–September30–34°CToo hot for a comfortable afternoon round, so green fees fall to their lowest. Workable if the group will take 7am tee times without complaining.
October–November20–27°CThe other peak, and the one most UK societies choose. Warm, dry, and coinciding with tour golf in the region.
December–February12–17°CCool but very playable, cheap, and quiet. January is the wettest month, though showers rarely last long. A good winter escape if you're not chasing shorts weather.

Where to Stay

The Bases Groups Actually Use

Sueno Hotels

The value tier. Two courses on site, full all-inclusive, and the cheapest way to get a week of golf in Belek.

Gloria & Kaya Palazzo

The mid-range workhorses. Golf on site, strong all-inclusive food and drink, and package prices that suit a whole society travelling together.

Cornelia & Cullinan Belek

A step up in comfort with the golf still attached. Where most UK groups end up when they want five-star without the Maxx Royal price.

Maxx Royal & Regnum Carya

The top end. The best two courses on the strip, ultra-luxury all-inclusive, and a price to match a fortnight elsewhere.

Before You Book

Worth Knowing

  • Price the package, not the green fees. A seven-night all-inclusive with three or four rounds bundled is almost always cheaper than the same hotel plus rack-rate golf, especially at the premium courses. "Unlimited golf" deals only pay off if the group genuinely plays five or more rounds in the week — for a normal society trip, a fixed-round package is better value.
  • Buggies are close to compulsory and cost extra. Budget roughly €40–€75 per round on top of the green fee at most Belek courses. Club hire is widely available at €20–€100 a set if some of the group would rather not fly with their own.
  • Don't book July or August unless the group means it. The cheap summer green fees look tempting until you're standing on the 14th at two in the afternoon in 34 degrees. Early tee times are the only sensible way to play Belek in high summer.
  • Green fees are quoted in euros, and tipping is expected. Bring euros in cash for caddies, buggy staff and the halfway house. We couldn't find a reliable published norm for golf-specific tipping rates in Belek, so ask your operator what's usual rather than guessing.
